How it works

The Y Loop™

Other apps give you answers. Answers are where curiosity goes to nap. Y runs a patented three-step loop that keeps you moving. Forward. Slowly.

STEP 01
Ask.

Ask Y anything

Why is the sky blue? Why do we have to go to bed? Why is it called a driveway? Y accepts all questions, including the ones adults are tired of.

STEP 02
"Why?"

Y asks you why

Powered by Snail Intelligence (S.I.), Y processes your question and responds with the single most powerful word in science. Then waits.

STEP 03
Repeat.

Repeat until smarter

Every "why" opens a new path. Keep going until you figure it out, run out of whys, or get called to dinner. Whichever comes first.

The origin story

The brainchild invention of Avie Abell of Knoxville, Tennessee.

Y began the way most great ideas do: with a kid asking "Why?" over and over until the nearest adult ran out of answers. Somewhere on the road between Knoxville and the Smokies, Avie Abell noticed something important. The questions were more fun than the answers.

So Avie drew a snail. The snail got a question mark. The question mark got a tagline. And now the whole thing has a headquarters, a roadmap, and a very confused patent office.

Y is Avie's brainchild, built on a simple belief: if you keep asking why, you keep moving. It doesn't have to be fast. It just has to be forward.
